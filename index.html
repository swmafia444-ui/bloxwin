Frequently Asked Questions

What is Coinflip and how does it work?

Coinflip is a 1v1 PvP game mode where participants place their bets by picking one side of the coin. Each player's chance of winning a particular match is +/- 50%. However, the player creating a coinflip is allowed to widen the range of value that can be used to join.

Yes, we don't require your roblox password for you to login hence making your account 100% safe and secure.

Provably Fair

What is Provably Fair?

BloxyWin uses a provably fair system to ensure that every coinflip outcome is completely random and cannot be manipulated by the site or any third party.

How does it work?

Each match generates a unique server seed and a client seed. The outcome is determined by combining these seeds with a nonce using a cryptographic hash function (SHA-256).

Before the match starts, the server seed is hashed and the hash is made public. After the match, the original server seed is revealed so you can verify that the outcome was fair.

How to verify?

You can use any SHA-256 hashing tool to verify that the hash of the server seed matches the one published before the match. If it matches, the outcome is provably fair.

For detailed verification steps, please contact our support team via Discord.

Why is this important?

Provably fair ensures that no one – not even the site operators – can predict or manipulate the outcome of any match. This gives you full confidence that you are playing a fair game.
